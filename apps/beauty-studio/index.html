02what it does

built around your face, not a trend.

Start with your structure.

Face shape, proportions, and the feature worth leading with. Eye shape and where liner belongs. Brow architecture, lip proportions, and frames that suit your bone structure.

Find your colors.

Your seasonal palette, your five best colors, and the neutrals that hold them together. Gold or silver. Colors for an interview, an evening, a wedding invitation. Hold a garment to your face and get a verdict.

See it before you commit.

Try a cut, a shade, a makeup look or an outfit on your own photo first — then decide. Hair length and color, natural to glam makeup, capsule outfits, a 30-day glow-up preview.

94 analyses, one library.

Face, skin, color, hair, makeup, outfits, style identity and glow-up planning across ten categories. Every result is saved as a report you can reopen while you shop, compare over time, or export as a card.

Nine languages.

English, Turkish, German, French, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Spanish and Dutch — interface and written reports alike, following your device language.

Your photos stay yours.

Photos are stored privately to your account, never shown to other users, and never sold. Delete a photo, delete every report, or delete your whole account from inside the app.

Beauty Studio is a styling tool — not medical, dermatological, or cosmetic-procedure advice. The analysis is generated by AI and can be wrong. Treat it as a well-informed second opinion, not a verdict. The app does not score attractiveness and never ranks you against anyone else.
